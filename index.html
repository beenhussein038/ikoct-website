About IKOCT

Every registered orphan deserves our care.

We believe that every registered orphan deserves safety, opportunity, education, healthcare and hope for a brighter future. So we hold ourselves to one measure: every orphan should be treated as though his/her parents were alive.

What we do

Our Programs

Education

Currently, we provide educational support to our registered orphans at Primary, Secondary and Tertiary levels of education; in which the orphans are provided with school fees, registration fees, school uniforms, books and stationery as well as examination fees for WAEC, NECO, JAMB, etc.

Healthcare

We settle medical bills of the orphans when the need arises.

Feeding

We provide orphans with foodstuff such as grains, vegetables, vegetable oil, sugar, meat, etc.

Clothing

The Trust provides, on annual basis, clothes to more than half of the orphans under its care.

Empowerment

We train orphans and their mothers in different skills acquisition programmes and other empowerment programmes annually for them to be self-reliant.
