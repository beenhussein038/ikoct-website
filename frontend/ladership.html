Professor Shehu Usman Abdullahi

mni

Professor Shehu Usman Abdullahi was born on 2nd February, 1951 at Ikara, the headquarters of Ikara Local Government Area of Kaduna State. He attended Ikara Primary School (1958-1960) and Soba Senior Primary School (1961-1963). He attended Provincial Secondary School (now Alhudahuda College), Zaria for his Secondary School education (1964-68) and Government College (now Barewa College), Zaria for his Higher School Certificate studies (1969-70). He obtained a Doctor Of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) degree from Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria (1971-76) and a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in the same field from University of Minnesota, USA (1979-82), specializing in Veterinary Urology. He attended the Institute for Policy and Strategic Studies, Kuru-Jos (1990)where he acquired the title of Member of the National Institute (mni). He also holds certificates in University Administration, Computer Appreciation, and Arabic Language. He joined the services of Ahmadu Bello University in 1977 and rose to the rank of Professor in 1989, a period of twelve years. He held the following positions at different times:

  1. Director Veterinary Teaching Hospital, ABU, Zaria (1988-1992)
  2. Deputy Vice-Chancellor, ABU, Zaria (1994-1995)
  3. Academy Provost, Nigerian Defence Academy Kaduna (1996-2004)
    4. and
  4. Vice-Chancellor, ABU, Zaria (2004-2009).
Profesor Shehu Usman Abdullahi served as Chairman/Member of several national and international committees. Currently, he is the Pro-Chancellor and Chairman of the Governing Council of Kaduna State University. Professor Abdullahi has over 100 scientific publications to his credit including in journals (national and international), textbooks, seminars and conferences. He discovered a new treatment for an animal disease called Struvite Urolithiasis (Stone Disease) in 1982. The treatment was approved for use in animals by the American Food and Drug Administration in 1983 and has since then been adopted for human beings. Professor Abdullahi has supervised several postgraduate students who graduated with PhDs and Masters degrees and is still teaching in ABU, Zaria. Professor Abdullahi is a holder of a National Honour, Officer of the Order of the Federal Republic (OFR). He is married and has ten children. Professor Abdullahi has been an ardent golfer since 1998 and was at different times a Member, Board of Trustees of Nigerian Defence Academy Golf Club, Kaduna Golf Club, and Zaria Golf Club. He is also involved in other community services.

Dr. Abdurrahman Sambo,

mni

mni

Abdurrahman Sambo was born on February 10, 1956 in Ikara LGA, Kaduna State. He qualified as a Medical Doctor in 1980, having earned an MBBS degree from Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria. He earned an MHPEd degree under the World Health Organization (WHO) fellowship from University of New South Wales, Sydney, Australia in 1992 and also earned a Certificate in Health Planning, Economics and Management as a Chevenning Scholar from Nuffield Institute for Health, University of Leeds UK in 1996. He was admitted into the National Institute for Policy and Strategic Studies, Kuru, Jos, 2003 and became a Member of the National Institute (mni). He began his career as a House Officer and rose to the post of a Registrar of Medicine at the Ahmadu Bello University Teaching Hospital, Zaria between 1980 to 1985. He later served variously in administrative positions at the National Universities Commission (NUC) between 1987 to 2006. Sambo`s wealth of experience has found him in several other administrative positions including Honourable Commissioner for Health, Kaduna State 1997 to 1999, member to several Committees and a pioneer member of the Governing Council of Kaduna State University between 2003 to 2006. He was appointed a General Manager with the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) in April 2006 and was appointed its Acting Executive Secretary between March 2012 and November 2013. He retired from the National Health Insurance Scheme in March 2014. He has since retirement, been engaged as Chairman, YUSAS Farms Ltd, Kaduna and Chairman Board of Trustees of Ikara Orphanage Charity Trust. He is happily married and blessed with 4 children.

Alh. Shehu Muhammad Shitu

Alhaji Shehu Muhammad Shitu was born on 15 may, 1952 in Ikara Local Government Area, Kaduna State of Nigeria. He attended Ikara Central Primary School (1958 - 1962) and Soba Senior Primary School (1963 – 1965) for his primary education. He also attended Government Secondary School Alhudahuda College Zaria (1966 - 1970) and Government Collage Kaduna (1971 - 1972) for his secondary school education, he obtained his Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration with specialization in marketing from the prestigious Ahmadu Bello University Zaria between in 1976. Alhaji Shehu also obtained Master’s degree in Business Administration in 1999 from the same institution. He began his career with French company, (CFAO) in 1977, where he was posted to Sokoto branch as the pioneer branch manager. In the year 1979, he left CFAO and joined Nigerian Tobacco Company (NTC). He later worked with many companies such as, Songhai Petroleum Company, Farida Investment and Finance Company and Ikara Food Processing Company. In the year 1991, he was at Industrial and Finance Company Ltd. (KIFC) as Assistant Investment Manager. Alh. Shehu was a member of the Board of Directors for several companies which included Kaduna State Housing Authority, Gaskiya Corporation Ltd. Zaria, Polyfibre Industries ltd., Kaduna, Zaria Industries Ltd. He also served as member of Revenue Generation Committee of the Kaduna State University and Council of Kaduna Chamber of Commerce, Industry, Mines and Agriculture (KADCCIMA), Kaduna. Alh.Shehu Muhammad Shitu is married and blessed with children.

Alh. Ibrahim Sambo

mni

Mal Ibrahim Sambo was born on 28th February, 1960 in Ikara, Ikara LG of kaduna state to the family of Late Alh Sambo Bambale (Malamin Asibiti). He attended LEA primary School Ikara from 1965 to 1971, Government Secondary School Zaria (present Alhudahuda college) 1972 to1976 and kaduna state college of Education Gidan waya, kafanchan 1981 to 1984 where he obtained the Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE). He participated in the National Youth Service Corps in 1984/85 at Science Secondary School kuru, Jos plateau State. Ibrahim proceeded to Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria in 1985 where he bagged a Bachelor of Education degree ( B Ed) in 1987. He further enrolled into Ahmadu Bello University Zaria, where he obtained a Postgraduate Diploma in Public Administration ( PGDPA); Master in International Affairs and Diplomacy (MIAD) and Master degree in Public Administration (MPA) in 1995, 2000 and 2007, respectively. He started his career as Lecturer 111 (Educational Psychology) with Kaduna State College of Education Gidan Waya in 1988. In 1992 he became a Lecturer 11 and was later converted to Administration cadre and rose through the ranks to the position of principal Assistant Registrar. He transferred his Services to Kaduna State Civil Service in 2000. Ibrahim held the following positions in the service of Kaduna State Government: Sole Administrator, Soba LG Council 1998 to 1999; Ag Director, Special Services Governor's Office 1999 to 2002; Chairman, Caretaker Committee, Ikara LG Council Sept,2002 to January,2003; Director, Administration and Finance, Ministry of works and Transport 2003 to 2008; Director Cabinet , Governor's Office 2009 to 2010; Director, Administration and Supplies Ministry of Health 2010 to 2012. In 2012, he was appointed a Permanent secretary and served in the following Ministries; State Universal Basic Education Board 2012 to 2016; Ministry for LG Affairs 2016 to 2017; and Ministry of Rural and Community Development 2017 to 2018. He voluntarily retired as permanent secretary in February, 2018. He was appointed a Permanent Commissioner, State Independent Electoral Commission (KADSIECOM) in February, 2018. He served the Commission from 2018 to July 2024 when he voluntarily resigned his appointment. Ibrahim is a member of the National Institute for policy and Strategic studies, kuru Jos SEC 37, 2015. He served as Chairman/Secretary/Member to more than thirty (30) important Committees and Commissions constituted by the State. He also attended several National and International Seminars and Conferences. He is married with five (5) children. His hobbies include reading and farming.

Dr. Shehu Idris

Dr. Shehu Idris is a lecturer at the Department of Microbiology, Kaduna State University. He was born on 12th January, 1981 in Ikara; the headquarter of Ikara Local Government Area of Kaduna State Nigeria. He attended Magaji Abubakar Primary School (1987 – 1993) and Government Secondary School, Ikara (1993 – 1999) for his primary and secondary school education respectively. Between the year 2000 and 2001, he enrolled for 1 year remedial science programme of Bayero University Kano, he also attended the same institution where he obtained his Bachelor of Science degree in Microbiology in the year 2006. Dr. Shehu participated in the one year mandatory national service program of NYSC at the State Ministry of Environment Calabar, Cross River State. He attended University of Technology Malaysia (UTM) from 2013 – 2015, where he obtained his Master’s degree in Biotechnology. Between the year 2018 and 2023, he attended and obtained his Doctorate degree (Ph.D.) with specialization in Environmental Microbiology from the University of Science Malaysia (USM). Dr. Shehu served as the Organizing Secretary of Muslim Student Society of Nigeria (MSSN), GSS Ikara in 1999, he also served as the President of Muslim Science Student Association (MUSSA) of Bayero University Kano in 2005/2006 Session. During his NYSC program, he served as the Chairman of the Da’awah Committee of the Muslim Corpers Association of Nigeria (MCAN) Cross River State (2007 -2008). He was the pioneer PRO of the Ikara Student Association (IKASA). He has been actively participating in community services. He is also a Co-founder of the Ikara Comprehensive Academy Dr. Shehu started his job career in 2011 as Graduate Assistant at the Department of Microbiology, Kaduna State University and rose to the rank of Lecturer I. He is still in the active service of the institution, he held many positions and served in several committees in the university. Dr. Shehu Idris is happily married and blessed with children.
